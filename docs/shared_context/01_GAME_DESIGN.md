# 01 GAME DESIGN

Status of everything below: **Designed (high level only). Nothing is implemented.**
Only the pillars given in the master instructions are recorded. No detail has been invented;
unknown items are listed as open.

## Pillars
- Space exploration.
- Spacecraft combat.
- Online cooperative adventure.

## Presentation
- Arcade flight model, third-person camera, stylized sci-fi look.
- PC first.

## Scope by phase (from the roadmap)
- Space environment: stars, nebulae, lighting, scale.
- Spacecraft and flight: initial fighter, arcade controls, acceleration, rotation, boost, overdrive, speed effects.
- Star systems and planets: orbital organization, planet classes, atmospheric transition, landing, surface exploration.
- Combat: weapons, shields, targeting.
- Co-op multiplayer: sessions, synchronization, shared worlds.
- AI: enemy and allied ships, patrols, encounters.
- Progression: missions, stations, discoveries, upgrades, economy.

## Open design questions
- Player count per session, story/mission structure, art direction details, target hardware (Q-002).
